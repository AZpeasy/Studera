Welcome to the Studera Grand Slam

Studera is an EdTech startup on a mission to make students powerful everyday — connecting them with mentors who have already walked the path they want to take.

The Grand Slam is our flagship fundraising sports festival: two days of Padel and Futsal, run by students, for the community. Every registration and ticket directly fuels Studera's work. Whether you're competing, volunteering, or cheering from the stands, you're part of it.
